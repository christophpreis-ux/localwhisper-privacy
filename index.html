Privacy Policy

Local Transcription - Whisper · Last updated: May 2026

No Data Collection

Local Transcription - Whisper does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All speech recognition and transcription happens entirely on your device using on-device AI models (Whisper). Your audio recordings and transcripts never leave your iPhone.

Microphone Access

The app requests microphone access solely to record audio for transcription. Recordings are processed locally and are never uploaded to any server.

Optional OpenAI Feature

If you choose to enable the optional OpenAI transcription feature, audio is sent directly to OpenAI's servers using your own personal API key. This data is subject to OpenAI's Privacy Policy. This feature is entirely opt-in and disabled by default.

No Analytics or Tracking

The app contains no analytics SDKs, no crash reporting tools, no advertising frameworks, and no third-party tracking of any kind.

No Accounts Required

No account, login, or personal information is required to use this app.

Data Storage

All transcripts and recordings are stored locally on your device only. You have full control over your data and can delete it at any time from within the app.

Children's Privacy

This app does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13.
